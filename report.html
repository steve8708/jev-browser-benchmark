Jev browser and computer use

Which system gets the task done?

Success is the score. Cost and time explain the tradeoff.

The result

Hybrid tied Luna on the scored success rates while being faster and cheaper in the simple browser lane.

Jev was dramatically faster, but its success rate dropped when a task required interpretation, typing, recovery, or verification.

The three lanes

What was tested

LaneTasksWhat it measures
Simple browser42 controlled tasksNavigation, clicking, forms, scrolling, small edits, and state checks.
Longer browser10 real-life-style workflowsFlights, restaurant reservation boundary, project tracker, Drive, Notion, Figma, mail, and app checkpoints. No irreversible purchase or send was submitted.

Important limitation

Browser tools are a separate check

Page inspection, accessibility information, and JavaScript evaluation are normal browser-tool capabilities for an LLM integration. A deterministic probe reached 14/14, and a Jev-plus-replay experiment reached 42/42. Those are not model-controlled Luna scores, so they are not folded into the headline table.
